# Artify — Handmade Art & Craft Store

## Project Overview

Artify is a UI/UX design project created in Figma for a handmade art and craft store. It showcases unique paintings, beautiful sketches, and handmade crafts created with creativity and care. The design provides a simple, elegant, and user-friendly experience for exploring artwork.

## Project Designs

### 1. Desktop Design

The desktop version is designed for a larger screen, providing a complete view of the Artify store with clear navigation and organized content.

**Features:**
- Header with Artify logo, navigation links, and Shop Now button.
- Hero section with the heading “Art That Makes Life Beautiful.”
- Explore Collection call-to-action button.
- Explore Our Collections section featuring Paintings, Sketches, and Handmade Crafts.
- Why Choose Artify? section highlighting Made with Love, Unique Designs, and Perfect for Gifting.
- Promotional section encouraging users to explore the store.
- Footer with brand information and copyright text.

**Frame Size:** 1440 × 2800 px

### 2. Android Mobile Design

The Android mobile version is designed for a smaller screen, with a vertically arranged layout that is easy to browse on mobile devices.

**Features:**
- Mobile header with Artify logo and menu icon.
- Hero heading, description, and Explore Collection button.
- Artwork image showcasing creative products.
- Vertically arranged category cards for Paintings, Sketches, and Handmade Crafts.
- Why Choose Artify? section with three feature cards.
- Promotional section with a Shop Now button.
- Footer with brand information and copyright text.

**Frame Size:** Android Medium preset in Figma

## Design System

- **Background Color:** `#F8F1E8` — Warm Cream
- **Primary Color:** `#A9513E` — Terracotta
- **Heading Color:** `#392D27` — Dark Brown
- **Body Text Color:** `#66574D` — Muted Brown
- **Secondary Color:** `#D9B99B` — Sand
- **Heading Font:** Playfair Display
- **Body Font:** Poppins
